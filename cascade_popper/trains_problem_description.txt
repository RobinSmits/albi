EAST-WEST TRAINS PROBLEM (Michalski & Stepp, 1977)
==================================================

ORIGINAL SOURCE:
- Paper: "Inductive Inference as Rule-Guided Transformation of Symbolic Descriptions" 
- Authors: Ryszard S. Michalski (1980)
- Dataset: UCI Machine Learning Repository (https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/103/trains)

PROBLEM DESCRIPTION:
This problem involves classifying trains as either Eastbound or Westbound based on their 
structural properties. It is a classic benchmark in Inductive Logic Programming (ILP) 
and machine learning.

STRUCTURE:
- Each train consists of multiple cars connected together
- Each car has the following attributes:
  * Length: short or long
  * Shape: closedrect, dblopnrect, ellipse, engine, hexagon, jaggedtop, openrect, 
           opentrap, slopetop, ushaped
  * Roof type: open, closed, jagged, arc, flat, peaked
  * Number of wheels: 2 or 3
  * Number of loads: 0 to 3
- Each car may carry loads of different shapes:
  * circle, triangle, rectangle, hexagon

RELATIONAL STRUCTURE:
  train --> has_car --> car --> has_load --> load
                         |
                         +--> car properties (length, shape, roof, wheels)

TASK:
Determine a concise decision rule that distinguishes Eastbound trains (positive class) 
from Westbound trains (negative class) based on the properties of their cars and loads.

The rule should describe what structural property or combination of properties 
makes a train belong to the positive (Eastbound) class.

EXAMPLE PREDICATES:
- has_car(Train, Car)      : Train has a car Car
- has_load(Car, Load)      : Car carries load Load  
- short(Car), long(Car)    : Car length
- roof_open(Car), roof_closed(Car), roof_jagged(Car), roof_arc(Car) : Roof types
- two_wheels(Car), three_wheels(Car) : Wheel count
- circle(Load), triangle(Load), rectangle(Load), hexagon(Load) : Load shapes

CITATION:
Michalski, R. & Stepp, R. (1977). Trains [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository.
